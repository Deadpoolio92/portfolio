1.0 Architecture, Pipeline Blueprint & Empirical Time Study

System Architecture • 40h → 7h

In performance marketing and customer acquisition operations, launching a tailored Google Ads campaign paired with a high-converting landing page traditionally requires ~40 hours of skilled manual labor. Unstructured onboarding calls, manual competitor research, character-bounded copy drafting, and tracking configuration create severe cognitive fatigue and operational drag.

Why Unconstrained "Prompt & Paste" Fails in Production

  • Character Limit Breaches: Raw LLMs routinely exceed Google's strict limits (30 characters for titles, 90 characters for descriptions), halting bulk uploads.
  • Negative Keyword Collisions: Ad groups compete against each other or trigger high-cost waste ("jobs", "free", "DIY").
  • AI Copy Slop: Generic platitudes fail direct-response emotional conversion hooks, depressing CTRs.
  • Compliance Bans: Hallucinated claims violate GDPR, financial, or healthcare policies, risking permanent domain suspension.

The Deterministic Pipeline Architecture

  • Structured Ingestion: 50 normalized fields extract psychographic pains before touching any model.
  • Programmatic Linters: Automated character boundaries (≤30/90c) and negative regex filters catch syntax errors instantly.
  • Non-Negotiable Human Gate: Human operator acts as Chief Quality Inspector, evaluating copy against CUB, SO WHAT?, and ELF rules.
  • Measured Business ROI: Operational turnaround time reduced from 40 hours to 7 hours (-82.5%) with zero policy defects.
